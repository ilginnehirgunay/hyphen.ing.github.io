Xiaomi Mi Stores
Ankara, Antalya & Istanbul, 2021 - 2023
Architecture Engineering Industrial Design
Ilgın Nehir Günay directed the construction site management for multiple Xiaomi Mi Stores across Antalya, Ankara, and Istanbul. Throughout the project, Nehir meticulously oversaw every phase of production, ensuring a seamless execution from the initial groundwork to the final launch day.
The foundational phases required rigorous oversight of structural demolition and the complex coordination of MEP arrangements. During this stage, Nehir also managed the precise installation of structural steel box sections, establishing a robust framework for the retail interiors.
As the spaces took shape, focus shifted toward meticulous interior finishing, encompassing precision ceramic floor tiling and comprehensive gypsum wall installations. Nehir coordinated the final plastering, painting, and the seamless integration of fixtures and furniture to bring the brand's distinct visual identity to life.
Ultimately, the successful completion of these three distinct store locations stands as a testament to highly adaptable and rigorous project management. By bridging the gap between raw construction and refined finishing, Ilgın Nehir Günay delivered functional, detail-oriented retail spaces across multiple major cities.

Ilgın Nehir Günay is an architect and designer focused on the intersection of industrial precision and the functionality. Through -ING, we lead a multidisciplinary practice that spans large-scale commercial construction to the intimate fabrication of raw stainless-steel and plywood furniture. By blending technical software tools with a hands-on approach, we strive to strip away the superfluous—creating spaces and objects that prioritize structural honesty and utilitarian purpose.
