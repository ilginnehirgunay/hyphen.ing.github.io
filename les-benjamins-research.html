Les Benjamins reSEARCH
Istanbul, 2022 - 2025
Initiated by Bünyamin Aydın, Founder and Creative Director of Les Benjamins, reSEARCH is a multidisciplinary creative collective. Ilgın Nehir Günay was tasked with establishing the foundational structure of the initiative, co-managing its development alongside Les Benjamins CMO, Gökçe Gürpınar.

Operating as both Project Manager and Lead Designer, Ilgın directed a six-person collective with expertise spanning architecture, spatial design, industrial design, graphic design, copywriting, and photography.
The collective's visual language was built from the ground up. Ilgın Nehir Günay designed the brand identity, establishing a cohesive system of iconography, logo design, and color palettes.
The inaugural project focused on mechanical street furniture, driven by a desire to reinterpret a daily, utilitarian object. The concept centered on designing a functional artifact capable of interacting seamlessly across both private and public social spheres.
The resulting "sliced bench" explores the physical tension between individuality and collectivity. Each modular slice is equipped with a chain, allowing it to lock into adjacent units. This mechanism introduces a focused connectivity with a deliberate, limited range of motion—emphasizing the structural potential of the individual components when linked together.
Driven by a commitment to material efficiency and sustainable practices, the production process prioritized zero waste. The team actively repurposed off-cuts from the primary bench fabrication to design and construct entirely new functional objects, including unique coffee tables and a rocking chair.
